\caption{A matrix showing the two standard short-run closures crossed
with the three financing closures. Each cell states the short-run condition
that defines the labour market of that economy and the financing condition
that closes its budget; the Leontief column additionally pins the
substitution elasticities. Notation: $L_{i}$ is sectoral employment,
$L_{i}^{\mathrm{cm}}$ the cost-minimizing labour demand and $\bar L$ the
labour endowment; $\Pi(p)$ is the consumption price index. Household
expenditure is $E^{h} = (1-\tau)\,w\sum_{i} L_{i} + F$, where $\tau$ is the
wage-income tax rate that finances the baseline government purchases
$g^{G}_{i}$ in every closure and the programme $g_{i}$ under F2, $F$ is the
net external transfer closing the external account
$S + T_{\mathrm{int}} + M - (I+X) = F + B_{\mathrm{gov}}$ (endogenous on the
mobile labour rows, identically zero on the fixed-wage rows), and
$B_{\mathrm{gov}}$ is the programme cost booked externally (F3 only; zero
otherwise). All symbols are defined in Section~\ref{sec:kernel} and
Appendix~\ref{app:model}.}
