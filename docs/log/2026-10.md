**Phase-4 tests — written, executed, green.** Two new files:
- `tests/test_numeraire_invariance.jl` — the mobile block is gauge-covariant:
  at a solution, re-gauging (p, w, F) → k keeps the physical block solved and
  moves only the CPI pin (reads k−1); off the solution the residual mapping is
  exactly degree-1 in (p, w), degree-0 in quantities and the real-wage labour
  row, and the pin is covariant (k·(r+1)−1); the fixed-wage closure pins w = 1
  instead of a CPI row, so scaling p alone breaks zero profit (no gauge freedom).
  14 assertions pass.
- `tests/test_supply_arm_recovery.jl` — non-circular: re-solves the scalar-BETA
  identification cells on the real table and pins (i) the wage is
  technology-determined and identical across η_s at a fixed magnitude, (ii) the
  implied ln L / ln w recovers η_s to 1e-6, (iii) the implied elasticity is
  invariant to the shock-magnitude ladder (the restriction the old circular test
  could not assert), (iv) the ALPHA control pins employment at 1, and (v) the
  re-solved numbers agree with the executed manifests to 1e-10 (golden
  regression). First run failed one assertion — my bug, not the model: I diffed
  `wage` across the *flat* magnitude×η_s vector, so I captured the
  magnitude-induced wage move (0.0043), not the η_s invariance. Fixed by
  collecting the wage at the fixed A1 = 1.2 magnitude. `Pkg.test()` then passed
