While being more encompassing in terms of the model space discussed this fully rewritten new version allows for discussing our original question in a more useful and better substantiated way.

Hence, it goes without question that we are extremely thankful for the constructive and direct feedback received from the reviewers, which guided us in rethinking the main setup of our narrative.
