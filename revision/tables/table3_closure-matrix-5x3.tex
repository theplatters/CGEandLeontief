\caption{The full closure matrix: the five labour-market closures (rows)

(labour market, the row), one for the financing of the programme (the
column). The standard CGE row and the $F1$/$F2$ column reproduce
Table~\ref{tab:closure-conditions}; the remaining rows add the labour-market
variations between the two poles --- behavioural supply on the real wage
(elastic labour supply), an uncapped extensive margin (fixed real wage),
the fixed-coefficient limit (Leontief), and immobile labour with sectoral
wages (no-reallocation). Notation follows Section~\ref{sec:kernel};
$\Pi(p)$ is the consumption price index, $E^{h}$ household consumption
expenditure, $T(p)$ the programme tax, and $B_{\mathrm{gov}}$ the externally
