\section{Sectoral responses in every closure}\label{app:variants}\label{app:sectoral-panels}

The figures below show what the nine distinct economies of the matrix make of the
program described in Section~\ref{subsec:sectoral} at the level of the sector.
Each panel refers to one cell (the collapsed cells of
Table~\ref{tab:results-matrix} share a panel). The left half compares, for each of
the seven program sectors, the size of the impulse with the resulting change in
gross output, in household consumption and in the sectoral price; the error bars
span the consumption elasticity $\sigma\in\{0.6,0.99\}$ with everything else
fixed. The right half places all 71 sectors in the output--price plane, with the
program sectors marked by their numbers, or, where no price moves, ranks them by
their output response.
